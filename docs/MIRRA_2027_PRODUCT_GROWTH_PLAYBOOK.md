# MIRRA 2027 · Human Connection & Retention Product Roadmap

**North star:** a real person completes something meaningful, finds a reason to share it, and returns. Not page views, message counts or synthetic tests.

## The loop we are building
**Curiosity → short original reflection → genuinely useful personal interpretation → a small practical lesson → a friend invitation or daily return → evolving identity.**

Everything begins free and without requiring an account. Real customers, not artificial social presence, determine what deserves more investment.

## Benchmark-to-product translation

| Benchmark | Mechanism observed | MIRRA implementation | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duolingo | Short lessons, characters, quests and progress without assuming all users respond the same | 16 short original lessons, 3 fictional editorial guides, Reflection Points, 3 daily missions | Personalizing learning from repeat behavior; accessible animations; cohort retention and feedback |
| Paired | Daily questions, two-person conversation exercises, private comparison | MIRRA Together, guest-first invite, private response comparison, clearly fictional solo demonstration | Recurring two-person question packs and consented partner follow-up |
| Dimensional | Multi-layered self-discovery and social comparison | 50-item IPIP Big Five, diverse exploratory experiences, private result history and two-person comparison | Deeper interpreted reports and opt-in friends graph |
| 16Personalities | Readable narratives that make results easy to discuss | Personalized narrative for six new journeys; printable/shareable report | More nuanced, grounded explanations for original nine journeys |
| Finch | Gentle repeat engagement and encouragement | Supportive daily reflections, device-local rewards without punishing missed days | Long-term adaptive guidance and user preference testing |
| How We Feel | Emotion vocabulary, tracking, contextual reflection | Daily emotional check-in and private journal | Explain patterns over time without asserting causal diagnosis |
| Partiful | Invite-first distribution without mandatory installation | Direct quiz deep links, free guest Together flow and copying messages | Invitation analytics from actual voluntary pairs |
| Boo | Optional personality-based connection and community | First Circle and private connections | Public social matching not built; requires real people and user demand |

## What's in the product today
- English-first editorial photography and layout. Free entry.
- IPIP-50 scored with published factor/reverse keys, explicitly separated from original exploratory prompts.
- 15 original exploratory genres (9 established + 6 new), 182 questions including the 50 Big Five items (precise inventory should be verified by tests), optional legacy collection.
- A deeply personal Together feature with a truthful fictional solo preview and real guest invitations.
- 16 short educational chapters across four learning paths; Ari, Rowan and June are fictional story guides.
- Device-local Reflection Points for one completion per journey, one per lesson, and one daily reflection per date.
- Progress & sentiment analytics with no quiz answer contents, distinct from real-person verification.
- Nine existing synthetic Chromium personas, extended by a 10th educational journey browser QA.

## Experiment ladder
**0–5 real volunteers**: observe whether users answer question one, reach a result and can describe its value. One-on-one feedback outranks A/B statistics.
**5–25**: test which first experience earns the most completions, invite click-through and candid result ratings.
**25–100**: compare short vs deep journeys, daily lesson recommendations, and voluntary return rate at D1/D7.
**100–1,000**: add opt-in small circles only after repeat pair interactions and moderation basics are shown to be valuable.
**Later**: consider social discovery, professional educational partnerships and revenue only if actual consented demand, safe operations and retention justify it.

## Measurement contract
- **Visits / browser IDs** are not verified users. Bots and QA must not be counted.
- **Started quiz** counts source visitor events. **First answer** measures engagement. **Question progress** captures furthest question number / total only (no answers).
- **Completed** means recorded end event, not scientific efficacy or satisfaction.
- **Result usefulness** is a voluntary 1–5 rating, shown only for groups of 5+; stated intent to return is not actual return.
- **Return** is observed browser revisit across days, not a guaranteed unique human or D7 cohort without sufficient window.
- **First Circle** is consented interest and not an activated account.
- **Verified real volunteer** requires independent source evidence of voluntary usage/feedback; never synthesize people.

## Design requirements
Natural English, carefully measured claims, premium human editorial art, accessible choices and sufficient contrast. Colors: warm ivory, deep forest, ink, muted copper. No bright purple/pink, inflated spheres or false member counts. Mobile 320/390, desktop 1440. Keep real consumer motivation above adding dozens of indistinct quizzes.
